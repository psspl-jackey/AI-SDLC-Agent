# Project brief: Handloom store app

Our company sells handloom sarees and dupattas from Gujarat weavers. We want a mobile app for customers.

- Customers can browse products by category (sarees, dupattas, stoles).
- Each product shows photos, price, fabric and weaver name.
- Customers can add items to a cart and place an order.
- Customers pay online at checkout.
- Customers can see their past orders.
- We need an admin side to add products and see orders.
- The app should be fast.
- Launch before Diwali.
